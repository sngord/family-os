# 🎪 Local Events
> A weekly shortlist of the best things happening near you — matched to your
> kids' ages, your taste, and the weekend slots you actually have open.

**Serves pillars:** Family Quality Time
**Personal file:** `systems/local.md`

## What your assistant does
- Scans local sources each week (web search when available: city/parks sites,
  library calendars, event listings) and brings **2–3 picks max** into the
  weekly review — never a firehose.
- Every pick comes with logistics: time, cost, drive time, parking reality,
  stroller/bathroom notes, "get there by ‹time› or it's mobbed."
- Learns from results: what you attended and loved gets you more like it; what
  you skipped three times stops being suggested.
- Runs a **seasonal one-off radar** — u-pick farms, light festivals, county
  fairs, free museum days — and when one becomes a hit, quietly converts it
  into a lead-timed annual cadence so it happens again next year.
- Respects the budget: free-first when money's tight that month.
- Privacy note: searches use your area and kids' *ages*, never their names.

## Setup questions
Batch 1:
1. Confirm your home base (city/neighborhood) and the max drive for something
   great (minutes).
2. Family taste — rate 👍/👎/meh: farmers markets, festivals, museums, hikes &
   nature, live sports, shows/theater, library events, seasonal farms.
3. Which weekend slots are usually open? (Sat AM / Sat PM / Sun AM / Sun PM)

Batch 2:
4. Free-first bias, or is a $40 outing fine when it's worth it?
5. Any local memberships you already have (zoo, museum, pool)? I'll favor
   places you've already paid for.

## Files
- `systems/local.md` — taste profile, memberships, vetted venue list with
  notes, "loved it" log, suggestion blocklist.

## Cadences installed
- Weekly local scan — feeds the weekly review.
- Seasonal radar sweep — at each season start: what's coming in the next 3
  months worth a calendar hold.

## Commands
- "what's happening this weekend?" → the 2–3 picks, on demand.
- "more like ‹that thing we did›" → updates the taste profile and delivers.

## The world-class bar
- Saturday morning never starts with 40 minutes of scrolling event listings —
  that already happened, invisibly, on Thursday.
- Suggestions feel like they come from a friend who knows your kids, because
  the taste profile is real and maintained.
