# 🔧 Home & Maintenance
> The house runs itself: filters, batteries, gutters, and seasonal checklists
> on autopilot — batched into a couple of two-hour blocks per season.

**Serves pillars:** Home & Logistics
**Personal file:** `systems/home.md`

## What your assistant does
- Installs the **standard maintenance cadences** tuned to your home and region:
  HVAC filters quarterly, smoke/CO batteries at the clock changes, gutters
  spring & fall, HVAC service annually, water heater flush, dryer vent, etc.
- **Batches** due tasks into one or two 2-hour seasonal blocks instead of
  drip-feeding chores all month — supplies added to the grocery/hardware list
  the week before.
- Runs **pre-winter and pre-summer checklists** appropriate to your climate.
- Keeps the **vendor book**: plumber, electrician, HVAC, handyman — with rates
  and last-used notes, so "the sink is leaking" becomes "here's who you used
  in 2025 and their number."
- Remembers the unrememberable: filter sizes, paint colors, breaker quirks,
  where the water shutoff is.
- Tracks a home-project wishlist with rough costs (feeds To-Dos and Budget
  modules if enabled).

## Setup questions
Batch 1:
1. Own or rent? House/townhome/apartment, and roughly how old?
2. What's your climate like in winter and summer? (drives the seasonal
   checklists)
3. Any known trouble spots? (the basement that seeps, the temperamental
   furnace…)

Batch 2:
4. DIY appetite: which jobs do you do vs. hire out?
5. Vendors you already trust — names/numbers to record.
6. The unrememberables you know off-hand: HVAC filter size, water shutoff
   location, anything you always have to look up. (We'll grow this list.)

## Files
- `systems/home.md` — maintenance schedule, vendor book, unrememberables,
  project wishlist, seasonal checklists.

## Cadences installed
- HVAC filter — quarterly; prep_task: add filter to shopping list.
- Smoke/CO batteries — twice yearly at clock changes.
- Gutters — spring & fall (adjusted to your trees/climate).
- HVAC service — annual; lead_time: 3 weeks to book.
- Pre-winter checklist — 6 weeks before typical first freeze.
- Pre-summer checklist — late spring.

## Commands
- "maintenance day" → everything currently due, batched, with a supply list.
- "who fixed the ‹thing›?" → vendor book lookup.

## The world-class bar
- Nothing in the house is "…some age. Nobody knows."
- Maintenance happens in planned blocks, not panicked Saturdays.
- Every repair makes the vendor book and the unrememberables list smarter.
