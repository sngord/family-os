# ⛺ Outings & Travel
> Camping trips, day trips, and vacations planned end-to-end — options,
> bookings, packing, calendar — while you just say yes.

**Serves pillars:** Family Quality Time (and Couple, for getaways)
**Personal file:** `systems/travel.md`

## What your assistant does
- On `/plan ‹trip›`, delivers the full package: **2–3 options with a clear
  recommendation**, cost estimate, booking checklist with deadlines, packing
  list, food plan, and calendar entries — you approve, it executes what it can
  and hands you the one booking click it can't do for you.
- Knows the **booking physics** of your region: popular campsites and cabins
  open reservations months ahead — those windows live in the lead-time engine
  with calendar blocks at opening time.
- Runs a **school-break radar**: spring break planned by mid-January, summer
  sketched by February, long weekends flagged a month out.
- Maintains **versioned packing lists** (car camping / hotel weekend / flying
  with kids) that improve after every trip via a 2-minute debrief.
- The debrief also feeds Traditions: a trip everyone loved becomes a candidate
  annual anchor.
- Watches weather the week before any outdoor plan and proposes the backup
  before you have to ask.

## Setup questions
Batch 1:
1. Outing appetite: how many day trips a month, and how many bigger trips
   (weekend+ / full vacation) a year feels right?
2. Camping family, hotel family, or both? If camping: tent/trailer, and what
   gear do you own?
3. Driving tolerance with the kids, honestly (hours).

Batch 2:
4. Where does the school-break calendar live? (link/school name — I'll track
   the dates.)
5. Top 3 places on the family bucket list right now.
6. Rough budget bands: day trip / weekend away / annual vacation.

## Files
- `systems/travel.md` — bucket list, booking-window intel for your region,
  packing list versions, trip debriefs, gear inventory.

## Cadences installed
- Quarterly outing target — every 3 months; lead_time: 6–10 weeks (set from
  your region's real booking windows once researched).
- School-break planning triggers — per break, at the lead times above.
- Reservation-opening blocks — per venue, as discovered.

## Commands
- `/plan ‹trip or outing›` → the full package described above.
- "trip debrief" → 3 questions, then lists and cadences quietly improve.

## The world-class bar
- The campsite is booked the morning reservations open, because the block was
  on the calendar and the site shortlist was ready the week before.
- Packing day is checklist-fast; nobody arrives without the sound machine.
- Trips end with a debrief so every trip plans the next one better.
