# ❤️ Couple & 1:1 Time
> Date nights that actually happen because the sitter's already handled — and
> one-on-one time with each kid that's deliberate, not accidental.

**Serves pillars:** Couple & Partnership, 1:1 Parent–Child Time
**Personal file:** `systems/relationships.md`

## What your assistant does
- Puts **date night on a rhythm** (e.g., twice monthly: one out, one at home
  after bedtime) and runs the full logistics chain for the "out" ones:
  reservation suggestion → sitter prompt 2 weeks ahead → confirmation check →
  calendar entry with the plan inside.
- Keeps an **at-home date menu** for the low-lift ones (new show + takeout
  upgrade, board game tournament, backyard fire) so "we're too tired to plan"
  stops meaning "we watched our phones."
- Tracks **1:1 kid dates**: a rotating rhythm so each parent–kid pair gets
  dedicated time monthly, matched to that kid's current obsessions — and
  quietly rebalances when one pair is going long. No guilt, ever; just "it's
  been a while since you two — here are two easy ideas for Saturday."
- Anniversary and each other's birthdays: planned at the lead time real plans
  need (4+ weeks), with ideas drawn from things you've each mentioned all year.
- All of it respects the budget module's comfort numbers if enabled.

## Setup questions
Batch 1:
1. Date night today: how often does it actually happen, and how often do you
   *want* it? Which nights work?
2. Out-date tastes: dinner spots, activities, "always wanted to try."
3. At-home date reality: after kids' bedtime, what sounds actually fun?

Batch 2:
4. Sitter logistics — (skips if Kids' Activities module handles your roster)
   who watches the kids for date nights?
5. 1:1 kid time: does each parent–kid pair have a ritual now? Any pairing
   that's been running on empty?
6. Anniversary date + how you like to mark it (big / small / alternating).

## Files
- `systems/relationships.md` — date rhythm + idea menus, sitter workflow, 1:1
  pairing tracker, anniversary/birthday playbooks, "they mentioned…" notes.

## Cadences installed
- Date night — per chosen rhythm; lead_time: 2 weeks (sitter + reservation).
- 1:1 kid dates — monthly per pair; lead_time: 1 week.
- Anniversary — annual; lead_time: 4 weeks.
- Partner birthdays — annual; lead_time: 4 weeks.

## The world-class bar
- Date night survives busy seasons because it requires zero planning energy
  from either adult — saying "yes" is the whole job.
- Each kid gets real solo time with each parent monthly, and nobody had to
  feel guilty to make it happen.
- The couple pillar never goes quiet for a month without a gentle flag.
