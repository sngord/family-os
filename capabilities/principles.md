# 🌟 Family Principles
> Put words to what your family stands for — built gently over a few weeks,
> then made visible in your home and lived out loud.

**Serves pillars:** Principles & Character
**Personal file:** `family/principles.md`

## What your assistant does
- Guides you to a short list (**7 or fewer**) of family principles through
  small prompts spread over weeks — never a forced one-sitting exercise.
- Harvests raw material from real life: phrases your family already says,
  moments captured in the inbox ("she shared her prize without being asked"),
  what you admire in other families.
- Runs a **2-minute principle prompt** inside the weekly review while the list
  is forming ("Finish this: in our family, when something is hard, we ___").
- When the list feels right, plans **ratification + art day**: pick the final
  wording as a family, then a make-it-visible project (framed print, painted
  canvas, hallway letters) with a supply list routed into the grocery order
  and a calendar block.
- Afterward, keeps principles alive: a monthly "principle in action" story in
  the review, and gentle links when planning ("this camping tradition is your
  'we do hard things outside' principle at work").

## Setup questions
One batch, unhurried — say explicitly that this module moves slowly on purpose:
1. Why do written principles appeal to you right now?
2. Any phrases your family *already* says that feel like you? ("we're a team,"
   "Hendersons don't quit," bedtime sayings…)
3. A family you admire — what do they seem to get right?
4. Twenty years from now, your kids describe growing up in your house in three
   words. What do you hope they say?

(Then let it breathe — the weekly prompts do the rest.)

## Files
- `family/principles.md` — candidate phrases with where they came from, draft
  list, ratified list with dates, principle-in-action stories.

## Cadences installed
- Weekly principle prompt — inside the weekly review (only while drafting).
- Draft check-in — monthly: is the list converging? Ready to ratify?
- Art day — one-time, once ratified; lead_time: 2 weeks (supplies + calendar
  block).
- Principle in action — monthly, forever: one observed story, one line.

## The world-class bar
- The principles are in your family's own words — assembled from things you
  actually said, not a values poster from the internet.
- The list is short enough that the kids can recite it.
- It shows up in decisions ("is the yes/no here one of our principles?") —
  that's the whole point.
