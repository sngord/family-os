# 🎄 Traditions & Celebrations
> The traditions your kids will describe in 30 years — protected, prepped weeks
> ahead, and evolving as they grow. Plus birthdays and gifts, handled.

**Serves pillars:** Traditions & Rituals
**Personal file:** `systems/traditions.md`

## What your assistant does
- Keeps the **tradition registry**: every annual and seasonal tradition with
  its month and its real prep lead time (costumes: 3 weeks; holiday cards:
  photos by early November; egg hunt supplies: 3 weeks out) — all wired into
  the lead-time engine so prep starts itself.
- Plans **birthdays** on a standard timeline: theme + guest list 6–8 weeks out,
  venue booked 6 weeks out (the good ones go fast), invites 4 weeks, cake &
  gifts 2 weeks — scaled to your party style and budget.
- Runs **year-round gift intelligence**: every time someone mentions a want
  ("she keeps talking about telescopes"), `/capture` it and it lands in that
  person's gift file — so gift season starts with a warm list, not a blank page.
- Suggests **tradition evolution** as kids age: what a 4-year-old loved needs a
  remix at 9 — nothing beloved gets dropped silently, and nothing outgrown
  limps on by default.
- Handles holiday logistics: hosting vs. traveling, extended-family
  coordination points, the shopping runway.

## Setup questions
Batch 1:
1. Traditions you already have and want protected — name + month. (Holiday
   rituals, birthday customs, first-day-of-school things, anything.)
2. A tradition you *wish* your family had?
3. Which holidays do you celebrate, and are you usually hosting or traveling?

Batch 2:
4. Birthday style per kid: party scale (friends bash / family dinner), rough
   budget, any fixed customs.
5. Gift philosophy: number/budget per kid per holiday? Grandparent
   coordination needed?
6. Whose birthdays and anniversaries beyond the household should I track?

## Files
- `systems/traditions.md` — tradition registry, per-person gift files,
  birthday playbooks, holiday logistics notes.

## Cadences installed
- One entry per tradition — annual, with its stated prep lead time.
- One entry per birthday — annual; lead_time: 8 weeks (party-scale) or 3 weeks
  (gift-only).
- Holiday gift runway — annual; lead_time: 6 weeks before each gift-giving
  holiday.

## Commands
- "gift ideas for ‹person›" → their warm list + 2 fresh suggestions in budget.
- `/plan ‹kid›'s birthday` → full party package via Outings-style planning.

## The world-class bar
- Prep windows open themselves; December feels like December, not like a
  second job.
- Gifts feel eerily thoughtful because the list was built in March, not
  December 20th.
- Traditions grow up with the kids on purpose.
