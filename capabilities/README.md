# Capability catalog

Each module is a self-contained spec: what the assistant does, the setup
questions it asks, the files it maintains, and the cadences it wires into the
lead-time engine. Enable any module during `/setup` or later with
**"enable ‹module›"**. Only enabled modules are loaded into context.

| # | Module | File | Great if… |
|---|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 | 📅 Calendar & Email | `calendar-email.md` | Your life runs on Google Calendar and school emails keep burying dates |
| 2 | 🍝 Meals & Groceries | `meals-groceries.md` | "What's for dinner?" is a daily negotiation and grocery orders take too long |
| 3 | 💰 Budget & Money | `budget-money.md` | Bills, subscriptions, and holiday spending sneak up on you |
| 4 | 💪 Health & Fitness | `health-fitness.md` | Workouts are the first thing cut, and nobody remembers the last dentist visit |
| 5 | ✅ To-Dos & Projects | `todos-projects.md` | Tasks live in three apps and your head, and house projects stall |
| 6 | 🎒 Kids' Activities & Childcare | `kids-activities.md` | You've missed a registration window before and never want to again |
| 7 | 🎪 Local Events | `local-events.md` | Weekends arrive with no plan and end with too much screen time |
| 8 | ⛺ Outings & Travel | `outings-travel.md` | You love trips but hate that planning them is a part-time job |
| 9 | 🎄 Traditions & Celebrations | `traditions-celebrations.md` | You want the traditions your kids will describe in 30 years |
| 10 | ❤️ Couple & 1:1 Time | `relationships.md` | Date night keeps not happening, and 1:1 kid time is accidental |
| 11 | 🔧 Home & Maintenance | `home-maintenance.md` | The furnace filter is… some age. Nobody knows. |
| 12 | 🌟 Family Principles | `principles.md` | You want your values written down and lived, not implied |

**Adding your own:** copy `_template.md`, fill in each section, and tell your
assistant to enable it. Ideas families have built: aging-parent care, pet care,
homeschool planning, garden calendar, volunteering rhythm.
